Support

Help with Vinyl for Apple TV.

The fastest way to get a question answered or a bug fixed is to write to support.vinyl.for.appletv@gmail.com. For reproducible bugs and feature requests, opening an issue on GitHub also works well and gives the conversation a permanent home.

When reporting an issue, including the following helps a lot:

  • Your Apple TV model and tvOS version (Settings → General → About).
  • The app version, shown in the app's Settings menu (the gear at the top of the gallery) and on its About screen.
  • A short description of what you were doing when the problem appeared.
  • If it concerns a specific record, the Discogs release ID (the number in the release URL).

Frequently asked questions

What do I need to use the app?

An Apple TV running tvOS 18 or later, and a free Discogs account with at least a few records in your collection. The app is read-only — it never modifies your Discogs data.

How do I sign in?

The first time you open the app, choose Sign in with Discogs and the TV shows a QR code. Scan it with your phone, log in to Discogs, and approve. Discogs then shows you a code: type it into the TV and choose Continue. The quick start guide walks through each step. From then on the Apple TV stays signed in until you sign out in the app's Settings menu or revoke access from your Discogs settings. If you delete the app and install it again, you sign in afresh.

Why is one of my covers missing or low-resolution?

The app looks every record up on MusicBrainz and shows the front cover the Cover Art Archive (a free, open image database run alongside MusicBrainz) has for it. When the Cover Art Archive has none, the app shows the Discogs image instead; Discogs allows community-submitted images and not every release has a hi-res one uploaded. If neither has a usable image, the cover stays blank. For a record the Cover Art Archive has no cover for, uploading a better one to Discogs is the fix: it reaches the app when you choose Refresh collection in the Settings menu. The check the app makes each time it starts only picks up newly added records.

Does the app play music?

No. Vinyl for Apple TV is a viewer for your physical collection. It shows artwork, tracklists, credits, and the room-ambient halo — but it never plays audio. The intent is to sit alongside your turntable, not replace it.

How does the halo / screensaver-suppression mode work?

When you open a record and swipe up, the cover takes over the screen and, after a few seconds, the app paints a halo of cover-derived colour blobs around it. While the halo shows, the app tells tvOS to keep the screen awake — handy if you want to sit with a record for an hour without the screensaver kicking in. Swiping on the Siri Remote's clickpad turns the halo off; leaving it alone for a few seconds brings it back. If you turn off Engage halo automatically in the Settings menu, a Halo button under the cover switches it on and off instead.

How does the app handle multi-disc releases?

Double LPs, triple LPs, and box sets appear with one position per side (A, B, C, D, and so on) on the side selector, the slider at the top of the tracklist. Swipe left or right on the record screen to move between sides; Discogs's disc-prefixed track positions (1-A1, 2-A1, etc.) are parsed automatically so nothing goes missing.

Where is my data stored?

Locally on your Apple TV. Your collection metadata is cached in SwiftData; cover images live in the Nuke image cache; your Discogs access token is in the Apple TV's keychain, on this device only, and is never synced through iCloud Keychain. Nothing is backed up to iCloud. Nothing leaves the device except the requests the app makes to Discogs, MusicBrainz and the Cover Art Archive. See the privacy policy for the full story.

How do I remove the app and its data?

Delete the app from the Apple TV home screen (focus the icon, press and hold the Siri Remote's clickpad, choose Delete App, then Delete; Offload keeps the app's data). That removes your library and the cached covers at once. Your Discogs sign-in stays in the Apple TV's keychain, which outlives the app; if you install the app again, its first launch removes that sign-in and you sign in afresh. To remove the sign-in straight away, sign out in the app's Settings menu before deleting the app. You can also revoke access at any time from your Discogs settings. There is no iCloud backup to clear.
