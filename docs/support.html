Frequently asked questions

01

Getting started

What do I need to use the app?

An Apple TV with tvOS 18 or later, a free Discogs account and at least one record with the format Vinyl. See Before you start.

Is the app on the App Store?

Not yet. See Get the app.

Does the app play music?

No. It shows covers, tracklists and the halo, and it never plays audio.

How do I sign in?

Choose Sign in with Discogs on the Apple TV, then follow the steps in Sign in.

02

Sign-in and account

The TV says Couldn't sign in.

The code may be mistyped or expired. Choose Try again on the TV to get a new code, then see Sign in.

My phone cannot scan the QR code.

Move closer to the TV, or turn up the room lights. If your phone still cannot scan it, use another phone or tablet with a QR camera, as described in Sign in.

Why did the app ask me to sign in again?

Discogs stopped accepting the app’s access, for example after you revoked it. Your library stays on the Apple TV. See Settings.

How do I disconnect the app from Discogs?

Revoke access in your Discogs application settings. The app returns to the sign-in screen the next time it reaches Discogs. See Your choices.

03

Your collection

Some or all of my records are missing.

The app shows only releases with the format Vinyl, so CDs, cassettes and digital releases stay hidden. If a genre is selected in the genre menu, only that genre shows, so choose All genres. See The toolbar.

A record I added on Discogs is missing.

Records you add appear after the next launch, or straight away after you choose Refresh collection. See Refresh collection.

A record I removed on Discogs still shows.

Removals appear only after you choose Refresh collection in Settings. See Refresh collection.

The gallery says No vinyl yet.

The app has no records with the format Vinyl to show. Add vinyl to your Discogs collection, then choose Refresh collection.

The TV says Couldn't build your library.

Choose Retry to build the library again. Choosing Continue anyway opens the gallery without a library. See Build your library.

The refresh pill says Refresh failed.

The reason appears on the pill for five seconds. Try again once the reason no longer applies, such as when Discogs is rate-limiting requests or the Apple TV is offline.

04

Covers

Why is a cover missing or low-resolution?

The app shows the Cover Art Archive cover when it finds one, and the Discogs cover otherwise. If neither has an image, the cover stays blank. See Covers that improve.

A cover changed after it first appeared.

That is expected. The app shows the Discogs image first, then swaps in a Cover Art Archive image once it finds one. See Covers that improve.

How do I get a better cover for a record?

Upload a better image to Discogs, then choose Refresh collection. This helps only when the Cover Art Archive has no cover for the record.

05

Halo and records

The halo does not appear.

In the Gallery and the Grid the halo appears after 30 seconds without a swipe. In the cover view you switch it on with a swipe right, unless you chose otherwise. Check that it is not switched off under Ambient halo in Settings. See To choose where the halo appears.

Where did the Halo button go?

It was replaced. In the cover view, swipe right to turn the halo on and left to turn it off. Engage halo automatically became Ambient halo in Settings, with a choice for each screen. See To choose where the halo appears.

Why does the screen stay on while I look at a record?

The app holds the screensaver off while the halo is on. See To choose where the halo appears.

A multi-disc record has sides C and D.

Discogs disc numbers continue the side letters, so disc 2 of a double LP is sides C and D. See The record screen.

The tracklist did not load.

The tracklist comes from Discogs when you open a record. If it fails, the record screen says so, and you can open the record again. See The record screen.

06

Data and removal

Where is my data stored?

On the Apple TV only. The privacy policy lists what the app stores and which services it connects to.

What happens to my library when I sign out?

Sign out and keep library keeps it on the Apple TV, and Sign out and erase library deletes it. The app asks which you want. See Settings.

How do I remove the app and its data?

Deleting the app removes the library from the Apple TV. The Discogs sign-in stays in the Keychain until you install the app again, so sign out first to remove it at once. See Your choices.

07

Report a problem

These details help most when you report a problem.

  • Your Apple TV model and tvOS version (Settings → General → About).
  • The app version, shown on the About screen: the gear at the end of the toolbar, then About.
  • What you were doing when the problem appeared.
  • If it concerns one record, its Discogs release ID: the number in the release page address.

Contact

For questions, bug reports and privacy questions, write to support.vinyl.for.appletv@gmail.com. Include the details from Report a problem.
